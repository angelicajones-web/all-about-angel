My Skills

Cybersecurity Fundamentals

Cybersecurity fundamentals focus on protecting computers, networks, accounts, and personal information from unauthorized access and cyberattacks.

Firewall Network Protection

Firewalls help protect computers and networks by monitoring traffic and blocking suspicious or unauthorized connections. I am learning how firewalls, secure networks, and access controls can help prevent hackers from reaching sensitive information.

Computer Forensics

Computer forensics involves identifying, preserving, examining, and documenting digital evidence from computers and other devices. I am interested in learning how investigators recover information, trace suspicious activity, and determine how a cyber incident occurred.

Threat Detection and Hacker Prevention

Threat detection involves recognizing suspicious activity, phishing, malware, ransomware, and other warning signs of a possible attack. I want to learn how to identify risks early and help prevent hackers from accessing personal information, accounts, and computer systems.

Secure Websites and User Protection

I want to create websites that are attractive, functional, and safe for the people who use them. This includes protecting user information, reducing security risks, and creating pages that are easy for everyone to understand and navigate.

Helping People Stay Safe Online

My goal is to help everyday people understand technology without feeling overwhelmed. I want to help users recognize scams, protect their accounts, use stronger passwords, and make safer choices online for themselves and their families.
